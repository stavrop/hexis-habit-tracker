# Nocturne Habit Tracker — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 22 July 2026_

Nocturne is a calm, private habit tracker for iOS and watchOS. It is built to keep
your data yours. This page explains, in plain terms, what the app does and does
not do with your information.

## The short version

- **We don't collect anything.** Nocturne has no accounts, no analytics, no ads,
  no tracking, and no third‑party SDKs. The developer never receives your data.
- Your habits and history live **on your device**, and — only if you leave iCloud
  sync on — in **your own private iCloud account**.
- Apple Health data is read **on your device** to auto‑track habits and is **never
  transmitted** anywhere.

## What data the app handles

- **Habits and logs** — the habits you create and their daily progress. Stored
  locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData.
- **iCloud sync (optional)** — if enabled, your habits sync through **your own
  private iCloud database** (Apple CloudKit) so your iPhone and Apple Watch stay
  in step. This data is stored in your personal iCloud account under Apple's
  terms; the developer has no access to it. You can turn sync off in Settings.
- **Apple Health (optional)** — if you link a habit to a Health metric (steps,
  water, calories, sugar, mindful minutes, and similar), the app reads those daily
  totals from HealthKit **on your device** to fill in that habit. This data is
  used only to compute your progress and is never sent off the device or shared.
  You grant and can revoke this access in the iOS Health app.
- **Reminders** — reminder times you set are used to schedule **local
  notifications** on your device. No reminder data leaves the device.
- **Focus timer / Shortcuts** — timer sessions and any Shortcuts/Siri actions you
  run act only on your local habit data.

## What we do not do

- We do **not** collect, transmit, sell, or share your personal data.
- We do **not** use analytics, advertising, crash‑reporting, or tracking services.
- We do **not** require an account or your email address to use the app.

## Bug reports and feature requests

If you choose to report a bug or suggest a feature from within the app, it opens a
GitHub issue in this repository. Anything you type there — and the basic app/device
info the template includes (app version, iOS version, device model) — becomes
part of a **public** GitHub issue. Please don't include personal information you
don't want to be public. Filing an issue is entirely optional.

## Your controls

- Turn **iCloud sync** on or off in Settings › Apple Health / Data.
- Grant or revoke **Health** access in the iOS **Health** app › Sharing.
- Manage **notification** permissions in iOS Settings.
- Delete any habit — or the app — to remove its data from the device (and, if you
  sync, from your iCloud).

## Children's privacy

Nocturne does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children, because
it does not collect data at all.
